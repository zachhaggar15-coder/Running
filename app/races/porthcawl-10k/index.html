Why this race may suit you

Race-day planning details

  • Course: Closed-road seaside 10K via Rest Bay, Trecco Bay, Coney Bay and the town centre, starting from Porthcawl Esplanade.
  • Arrival and start: 10:00 on Porthcawl Esplanade; junior races follow from 12:15.
  • Travel: Pre-bookable town parking has limited capacity; allow for road closures and follow event signage from M4 junction 37.
  • On-site support: Bag storage on The Portway, on-course water, pacers and an event village.
  • Entry and race-day rules: Race number with embedded timing chip is posted before the event. Buggies are restricted to the Fun Run.

Decisions before you enter

  • Ask the organiser for the overall and any intermediate cut-offs if pace affects your choice.
