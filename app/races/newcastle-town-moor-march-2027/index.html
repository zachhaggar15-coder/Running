Why this race may suit you

A city park race with several distances and good Metro access. Multi-lap navigation and compulsory on-day pack collection are the main practical details.

Race-day planning details

  • Course: Flat multi-lap park route on tarmac and compact gravel, with distance splits.
  • Arrival and start: 10K 09:30; half 09:35; 5K 09:55; juniors 12:00.
  • Climbing: 45m on 10K route
  • Travel: No dedicated parking or vehicle access to village; Haymarket Metro is about a 10-minute walk.
  • On-site support: Bag drop, chip timing and event village; all runners collect packs on race day.
  • Entry and race-day rules: 10K and half pack collection 08:00–09:00, 5K 08:30–09:30; follow split signs carefully.

Decisions before you enter
