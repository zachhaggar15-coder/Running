Why this race may suit you

Published ascent and checkpoints suit experienced trail runners.

Race-day planning details

  • Course: Approximately 100 miles across Bannau Brycheiniog from Abergavenny Castle to Llangadog, via the Black Mountains, Pen-y-Fan and Carmarthen Fans.
  • Arrival and start: 06:00 from Abergavenny Castle, NP7 5EE; registration and mandatory tracker collection precede the start.
  • Time limit: 40 hours, with strict checkpoint cut-offs
  • Climbing: 6,143m ascent
  • Travel: Point-to-point finish at Llangadog RFC. Optional organiser return transport is available; plan crew and finish collection.
  • On-site support: Ten checkpoints with food and drink, medical support and live GPS tracking.
  • Entry and race-day rules: Minimum age 20; prior organised 50-mile ultra and mountain experience required. Self-navigation with map and compass is expected.

Decisions before you enter

  • Plan the journey from the finish as well as arrival at the start.
