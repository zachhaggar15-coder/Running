Why this race may suit you

The 273m half ascent makes this a demanding park course. Multiple entrances and a walk to the start need to be built into arrival time.

Race-day planning details

  • Course: Undulating tarmac park paths; water and toilets about every 5km, except no course water for 5K.
  • Arrival and start: Half 09:30; 10K 10:00; 5K 10:10; juniors 12:00.
  • Climbing: 273m on half-marathon route
  • Travel: Pay-and-display parking; Heaton Park or Bowker Vale Metrolink stops with a walk to start.
  • On-site support: Bag drop, toilets and chip timing; village opens 08:00.
  • Entry and race-day rules: Half pack collection 08:15–09:15. Allow 10–20 minutes' walk from park entrances.

Decisions before you enter

  • Select your exact distance or route before comparing ascent, cut-offs and start arrangements; these can differ within one event.
  • Ask the organiser for the overall and any intermediate cut-offs if pace affects your choice.
  • Recheck the entry conditions and final participant instructions for your chosen distance.
