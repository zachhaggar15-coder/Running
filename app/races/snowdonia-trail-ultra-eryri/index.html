Race-day planning details

  • Course: Mountain route from Llanberis up toward Yr Wyddfa, through remote trails and the Dinorwig slate landscape, finishing in Llanberis.
  • Arrival and start: Usually 05:00 from Llanberis; the confirmed time and registration arrangements will appear in final instructions.
  • Time limit: 11 hours 30 minutes, with staged course cut-offs
  • Climbing: Approximately 2,300m ascent
  • On-site support: Feed stations
  • Entry and race-day rules: Minimum age 20, mandatory mountain kit and staged cut-offs; late arrivals at two course splits are diverted to the marathon route.
