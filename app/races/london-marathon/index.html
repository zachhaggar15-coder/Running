Why this race may suit you

Course and setting

From Greenwich and Blackheath via Cutty Sark, central London and the Thames to The Mall; the same route is planned on both 2027 days.

Race-day arrival

Greenwich/Blackheath. Saturday is expected to start about 09:00 and Sunday about 09:30; individual wave times will be issued later.

Entry and eligibility

2027 Saturday and Sunday allocations cannot be exchanged. Saturday finish closes at 17:30; the Sunday secondary finish closes at 23:59:59.
