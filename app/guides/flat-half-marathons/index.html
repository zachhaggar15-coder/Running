Which half marathons have a published climb figure?

A flat label can hide a meaningful climb. These road half marathons have course-specific ascent figures in the reviewed organiser material. Compare the figure and the route description before deciding whether one suits a personal-best attempt.

Newbury lists 30m for its half. East Yorkshire lists 30m for the 10km only, so its half is included as a useful contrast rather than a proven flat half.

  • Newbury Racecourse Half Marathon 10K and 5K

    Rail-accessible racecourse event with free parking; drivers must approach from the signed side of Racecourse Road.
