Races with useful arrival and departure details

A start near a station does not always mean an easy race-day journey. These examples show why parking restrictions, separate starts and point-to-point finishes deserve their own check.

Public transport timetables and road closures can change. Confirm the event instructions and the actual service on your race date.

  • Men's 10K Glasgow

    Point-to-point Glasgow course with an important transport trap: the subway does not open before its 09:30 start.

    Distance
    10km
    Date
    20 June 2027
    Cut-off
    1 hour 30 minutes
    Travel
    Partick rail about half a mile from start; no Riverside Museum parking; Sunday subway starts too late for race

  • Newcastle Half Marathon and 10K

    Closed-road country lanes around Newcastle Airport; the stadium parking closure materially changes where faster runners should park.

    Distance
    Half marathon / 10km
    Date
    11 July 2027
    Cut-off
    Not confirmed
    Travel
    Kingston Park and Bank Foot Metro within five minutes; stadium car-park exit closed 08:00–12:00

  • Carsington Water Trail Half Marathon and 10K

    The 10km uses a separate Millfields start and finishes at the visitor centre, leaving a 25-minute walk back to its start car park.

    Distance
    Half marathon / 10km
    Date
    10 April 2027
    Cut-off
    Not confirmed
    Travel
    Paid visitor-centre and Millfields parking; 10km starts 1.3 miles from shared finish

  • Manchester to Liverpool Ultra 50

    Low-climb point-to-point ultra following waterways; travel between Manchester and Liverpool is a major planning item.

    Distance
    50 miles
    Date
    26 March 2027
    Cut-off
    14 hours; finish by 20:00
    Travel
    Starts at Imperial War Museum North and finishes at North End Sports and Social Club, Liverpool

  • Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park 5K and 10K

    Flat park race with step-free Stratford access and water each lap; the park remains open to other visitors.

    Distance
    5km / 10km
    Date
    7 February 2027
    Cut-off
    Not confirmed
    Travel
    Stratford and Stratford International provide rail access; paid Westfield parking

How to compare these races
