Trail ultras with published cut-offs

An overall limit tells only part of the story. Intermediate aid-station deadlines, ascent and point-to-point transport can be more important for deciding whether an ultra fits your experience.

The figures here belong to the listed distance and edition. Check the official rules for intermediate cut-offs, qualification and kit before entering.

  • Hundred Hills 50km

    Two 25km Chiltern loops make the race suitable for crews who want to see runners at halfway; ascent totals about 1,200m.

    Distance
    50km
    Date
    20 March 2027
    Cut-off
    9 hours
    Travel
    Start and finish at Stonor Park; check race travel notes

  • North Downs Way 50

    Point-to-point North Downs Way race with varied chalk, woodland, mud and sand rather than one uniform trail surface.

    Distance
    50 miles
    Date
    15 May 2027
    Cut-off
    13 hours plus intermediate cut-offs
    Travel
    Farnham Leisure Centre HQ; finish near Knockholt Pound Village Hall, collection parking at Coolings Garden Centre

  • South Downs Way 50km

    A 50km South Downs crossing that ends after a descent from Butser Hill, with a separate finish venue from the start.

    Distance
    50km
    Date
    12 June 2027
    Cut-off
    9 hours
    Travel
    Point-to-point; arrange travel between Matterley Bowl and Queen Elizabeth Country Park

  • Thames Path 100

    A comparatively low-climb 100-mile point-to-point route, but riverside conditions and a 30-hour limit still demand preparation.

    Distance
    100 miles
    Date
    1 May 2027
    Cut-off
    30 hours, with aid-station cut-offs
    Travel
    Richmond station is near the start; finish at Queen's College Sports Ground, Oxford

  • Autumn 100

    Four 25-mile out-and-back sections return repeatedly to Goring, simplifying crew planning but adding up to 100 miles.

    Distance
    100 miles
    Date
    16 October 2027
    Cut-off
    28 hours with aid-station cut-offs
    Travel
    Four spurs return to Goring race hub; check village access and parking

  • Ultra Wales 50

    Southern Eryri mountain ultra including Cadair Idris; the organiser marks the 2027 ascent figure provisional.

    Distance
    51 miles
    Date
    19 June 2027
    Cut-off
    20 hours
    Travel
    Starts at Coed-y-Brenin Visitor Centre; confirm finish transfer

How to compare these races

Read each profile for course, start, eligibility and facilities, then follow its organiser link for the final instructions. Unknown values remain unknown; the listed dates and logistics should be rechecked before booking.
