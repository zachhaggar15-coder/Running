# Three pilot profile drafts — not for publication

Checked 4 November 2026. These are short, original factual drafts built from official pages to test what an independently useful profile might contain. Details can change; publication would require a final edition-specific review. No organiser text, imagery or map has been reproduced.

## South Downs Way 50 — 10 April 2027

The [organiser's 2027 page](https://www.centurionrunning.com/races/south-downs-way-50-2027) describes a point-to-point 50-mile race from Worthing to Eastbourne, with 85% trail and 15% road. It lists roughly 1,750 m of ascent, a 13-hour overall limit and intermediate aid-station cut-offs. The event starts at 08:30. Runners must have completed at least a marathon-distance race within that race's time limit since 1 January 2022; the organiser specifies that this is an entry qualification. The page says the course is marked and that support includes aid stations and medical cover. The advertised standard entry price is £118, with an affiliation distinction.

**Planning implications:** This is a long, mostly trail point-to-point course. The overall time limit is not the only pacing constraint; check the organiser's intermediate cut-off table before entry. A runner planning travel should allow for different start and finish towns.

**Unknown for this edition in the reviewed source:** precise public-transport plan, parking instructions, bag-drop arrangement, headphone rule and an exact independently measured ascent method. The organiser's ascent is published as its own figure. [Official entry and latest details](https://www.centurionrunning.com/races/south-downs-way-50-2027).

## Inverness Half Marathon — 14 March 2027

The [organiser's 2027 event page](https://caledoniangroupevents.co.uk/index.php/inverness-half-marathon/event/half-marathon) places the start beside the River Ness on Bught Road. The route runs through Inverness, has an early climb and some rolling middle miles, and finishes with a lap of the track at Queen's Park Stadium. The organiser states a 3-hour-30-minute cut-off, with some roads starting to reopen from 13:30. It lists a baggage drop at Inverness Leisure from 09:30 to 10:45, four on-course water stations at approximately miles 3, 6, 9 and 11.5, and a High5 gel station.

**Planning implications:** The cut-off policy also describes how marshals guide slower runners onto pavements when roads reopen. The early climb means the course should not be presented as uniformly flat, even though the finish is comparatively level.

**Unknown for this edition in the reviewed source:** a numeric elevation-gain figure, specific runner parking arrangements and a recommended public-transport route to the start. [Official entry and latest details](https://caledoniangroupevents.co.uk/index.php/inverness-half-marathon/event/half-marathon).

## Snowdonia Trail Ultra Marathon Eryri — 11 July 2027

The [organiser's 2027 ultra page](https://alwaysaimhighevents.com/events/snowdonia-trail-marathon-eryri/snowdonia-trail-ultra-marathon-eryri) describes a redesigned route of approximately 56 km with about 2,300 m of ascent, starting and finishing in Llanberis. It climbs high onto Yr Wyddfa, later passes through Dinorwig Quarry and uses mountain paths, tracks and wooded sections. The organiser publishes staged cut-offs: 21.8 km in 4 hours 40 minutes, 28 km in 6 hours, 38.5 km in 8 hours and 45.3 km in 9 hours 15 minutes. At two points, late runners are directed onto the marathon course. Mandatory kit is specified and spot checks are planned.

**Planning implications:** The 2027 route is explicitly redesigned, so older GPX tracks or ascent values should not be assumed current. Intermediate cut-offs and required kit matter more here than a single headline finish-time limit.

**Unknown for this edition in the reviewed source:** an independently verified ascent measurement, runner parking, public-transport instructions and whether every feed-station provision is final. [Official entry and latest details](https://alwaysaimhighevents.com/events/snowdonia-trail-marathon-eryri/snowdonia-trail-ultra-marathon-eryri).
